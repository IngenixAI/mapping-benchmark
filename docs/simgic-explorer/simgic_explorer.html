simGIC on the ontology graph

Click a term, then a second one. simGIC is Σ IC(A ∩ B) / Σ IC(A ∪ B) over the two ancestor cones — the term itself plus everything above it — so the page paints exactly the sets the ratio is taken over: shared, only A, only B. A node's area grows with its information content: the root is a dot and weighs nothing on either side of the ratio, a leaf is fat and carries the score.

Information content is −ln(terms at or below t / terms), rarity as the ontology was written, so a score never moves with the mix of samples under test.

The graph is MONDO restricted to the terms this benchmark reaches — every annotated term plus its full closure, so a drawn cone is the whole cone the score sums over. MONDO is a DAG: a term can sit under several parents, and a cone is a set, so a term reached twice is still counted once. Rows are longest-path depth. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.

With one term picked, every other term is scored against it and the graph dims by that score — the brighter a node, the more simGIC it shares with your pick, so the terms worth comparing stand out of the hundreds that do not. The panel ranks them; click a row (or the node) to make it B. Every pair at once, clustered into blocks, is the matrix.
